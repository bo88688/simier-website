From the Provencal afternoon to a California morning — a good sofa is the soul of a first home. Each piece shaped by hands that have spent decades learning what comfort truly means.

Sofas, Chairs & Armchairs

Every seat we make begins with a frame built to outlast its owner. Solid hardwood, reinforced joints, and a quiet obsession with the angle of a backrest — the difference between sitting down and settling in.

Our upholstery is sourced from Italian mills and cut in-house. Foam densities are specified per cushion, per chair, per use. Because the way you sit in a dining chair is not the way you sink into a sofa.
