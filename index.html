Global Presence

Three Seas
One Vision

South of France — Sud de la France

Provençal light through shuttered windows, falling across a SIMIER linen sofa. From Montpellier to Marseille, our pieces inhabit student apartments where afternoon sunlight is a way of life.

California Coast — United States

Los Angeles, Santa Barbara, Irvine — in the apartments that surround California's university towns, SIMIER is the first piece of furniture in the first real home. Sun, coastline, and a well-made sofa: the essentials of young Californian life.

Bosphorus — Türkiye

From Istanbul to Ankara, spanning two continents. The Turkish sensibility for colour and texture finds its match in every SIMIER piece — a natural affinity between craft traditions that span East and West.
